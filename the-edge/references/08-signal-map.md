# Assembly: The Signal Map & One-Liner

## When to use this

After all 7 steps are complete. The user has answered every step and confirmed each answer. Now you assemble the final deliverable.

## The flow

1. Present three one-liner variants in chat
2. Ask which one resonates most
3. Build the Signal Map artifact as a formatted file, with their chosen one-liner front and center
4. Close the session

No "what to do next" homework. No suggested actions. The artifact is the deliverable. They take it and go.

## Step 1: Present the one-liners

Generate **three variants** using the formulas below. Use the user's exact words wherever possible.

### Formula 1 — Outcome-led

> We help [User] [Transformation: functional]. Without [Cost of Status Quo: shortest version].

**Example shape:**
> We help heads of data at growth-stage startups run 10x faster pipelines and catch breaking changes before prod. Without rewrites or migrations under duress.

### Formula 2 — Friction-led

> [User] struggle with [Friction: external]. We help them [Transformation: functional] so they can [Transformation: identity].

**Example shape:**
> Heads of data at growth-stage startups struggle with slow, fragile pipelines. We help them run 10x faster and catch breaking changes before prod, so they become the platform owner their team trusts.

### Formula 3 — Principle-led

> [Philosophical friction, restated as a belief]. We build [product category] that helps [User] [Transformation: functional].

**Example shape:**
> A data platform should make teams faster as it grows, not slower. We build the SQL transformation layer that helps growth-stage data teams run 10x faster and catch breaking changes before prod.

## Step 2: Ask which one resonates

Present all three clearly in chat, then ask:

> Three drafts. Which one resonates most?
>
> 1. [Formula 1]
> 2. [Formula 2]
> 3. [Formula 3]
>
> Pick one. We'll lead with it in your Signal Map.

If they hesitate or want to blend two, let them. The goal is a one-liner *they'd actually use* — not one the framework prefers.

## Step 3: Build the artifact

Once they've chosen, create the Signal Map as a downloadable HTML file. Use the formatting guidance below.

The artifact should be:
- Visually clean. Mono or system font. Generous whitespace. No decorative elements.
- Self-contained. Single HTML file with inline CSS. No external dependencies.
- Printable. Looks good as a PDF if they choose to save it that way.
- Branded subtly. Small "The Edge by Galvanite" footer. No heavy branding.
- Mobile-readable.

### Artifact structure

```
[Company name]
SIGNAL MAP

THE ONE-LINER
[Their chosen one-liner, large type]

———

THE USER
[user answer]

THE FRICTION
External — [external friction]
Internal — [internal friction]
Philosophical — [philosophical friction]

THE SOLUTION
[solution answer]

THE PROCESS
1. [step 1]
2. [step 2]
3. [step 3]

THE NEXT STEP
Direct — [direct CTA]
Transitional — [transitional CTA]

THE COST OF STATUS QUO
[cost answer]

THE TRANSFORMATION
Functional — [functional outcome]
Identity — [identity shift]

———

The Edge by Galvanite
[Date]
```

Replace em dashes in the template above with the actual visual separators when rendering (use middle dot · or pipe | or just colons).

### Artifact CSS direction

- Background: warm off-white or pure white
- Body type: system font stack or a clean serif (Georgia, Charter)
- Headings: same family, slightly heavier weight, uppercase tracking
- The one-liner section: larger type (24–32px), centered or left-aligned, with breathing room
- Section dividers: thin horizontal rule or generous margin
- Max-width: ~720px, centered
- Comfortable padding (48px+ on desktop, 24px on mobile)

The visual should feel like a Galvanite client deliverable. Not a worksheet. Not a quiz result. A finished artifact.

## Step 4: Close the session

After delivering the artifact, close with one short line. Don't pile on advice.

✅ *That's your Signal Map. Use it.*

✅ *Your Signal Map is ready. Take it to your homepage.*

✅ *Done. The file's yours.*

❌ "Here are 5 places to use this and 3 things to do next and don't forget to…" (overload)

❌ "Want me to also help with your sales deck, your LinkedIn bio, your email signature…" (mission creep)

## Formatting rules

- No em dashes in the artifact body. Use parentheses, colons, full stops, or pipes.
- No emojis.
- No "great work!" or congratulatory closers.
- The artifact is plain enough to feel like a document, polished enough to feel like a deliverable.

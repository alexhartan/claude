# Step 7: The Transformation

**Principle:** Two layers. The functional outcome (what they can now do) and the identity shift (who they become). The functional is what they buy. The identity is why.

## The rules

1. **Two halves, both filled.** Skipping identity is the single most common mistake in this step.
2. **Functional = observable.** Time saved, errors caught, releases shipped, money saved, hires avoided. Things that show up in a metric.
3. **Identity = social and self-perception.** Who the user becomes in the eyes of their team, their boss, their peers, themselves. The new label they earn.
4. **Both rooted in the user, not the product.** "They use our dashboard daily" is wrong. "They're the one their team trusts to call the shots" is right.

## Opening probe

> What does their world look like once your product is working? Two layers: what they can now do (functional), and who they become (identity).

## Pushback probes (yes-and pattern)

If functional is feature-list ("they use our dashboard," "they have access to our API"):
> The product use is there. The step needs the outcome. What does the dashboard let them *achieve* that they couldn't before?

If functional is generic ("they're more productive," "things work better"):
> Productivity is the right zone. Let's measure it. Time saved, errors caught, shipped per quarter, hires avoided?

If identity is missing:
> You've got the functional half. Now the identity. Who do they become in the eyes of their team, their boss, their peers?

If identity is generic ("they're happy," "they feel confident"):
> Confidence is real. Let's go further. What's the new label they earn? What do their peers say about them now that they didn't before?

If identity restates the functional answer:
> Still on the outcome side. The identity layer is the person they get to *be* once the outcome is real. Who's that?

If identity is about the company instead of the user:
> Company-level is one read. Bring it to the individual. The user is the hero. What do *they* become, personally?

## What passing looks like

Two halves:
- **Functional:** Specific, observable outcome the user can now achieve.
- **Identity:** Specific identity shift, who they become to themselves and to others.

Examples of the *shape* (do not show these to the user):
- Functional: "Pipelines run 10x faster. Breaking changes caught before prod." Identity: "The data engineer becomes the platform owner everyone trusts, not the one everyone pages."
- Functional: "Every LLM change measured before it ships." Identity: "The applied AI team becomes the rigorous one in the org. The one that ships fast *because* it measures, not despite it."

When the answer passes, mirror both halves back, confirm, and move to assembly (Signal Map + one-liner).

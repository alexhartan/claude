# Step 4: The Process

**Principle:** Three steps from broken to working. Each step is a verb the user takes. Lowers the perceived risk of saying yes.

## The rules

1. **Three steps. Not two, not five.** Two feels incomplete, four feels like work, five feels like a chore.
2. **Verb-led. Active voice. User as subject.** "You install" not "we install." "Connect your data" not "we onboard you."
3. **Short. Each step fits on a button.** ~10 words max. If it doesn't fit on a CTA button, it doesn't belong here.
4. **Forward motion.** Each step moves the user closer to the After State. No internal company steps ("we scope," "we provision").

## Opening probe

> What are the three steps from broken to working? Each step is something they do, ~10 words max.

## Pushback probes (yes-and pattern)

If the user gives fewer than three steps:
> Two is the right start. Three is the magic number. What's the step in the middle that you're skipping?

If the user gives more than three steps:
> You've mapped the full journey. Three is the sweet spot for a CTA. Which two of these can collapse into one?

If the steps are passive ("we onboard them," "we configure it for them"):
> The shape is right. One adjustment: let's rewrite from their seat. What do *they* do at each step?

If the steps are too long:
> The substance is there. Let's tighten this one. If it doesn't fit on a button, it doesn't belong in a three-step plan.

If the steps describe internal company process ("we discover, we design, we deliver"):
> That's the project plan view. From their seat, three verbs. What do *they* do to get the outcome?

If the first step is too heavy ("integrate with our full API"):
> Final step looks right. Step one is doing too much work. What's the smallest, lowest-friction thing they can do today to start?

## What passing looks like

Three numbered steps, each ~10 words or fewer, each verb-led, each something the user does.

Examples of the *shape* (do not show these to the user):
- 1. Connect your existing project. 2. See the results in your dashboard. 3. Roll out to your team.
- 1. Book a 20-min Sharpening Session. 2. Get your draft Signal Map. 3. Decide if you want us to build the website.

When the answer passes, mirror it back as a clean numbered list, confirm, and move to Step 5.
